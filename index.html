Scoot · Payments

Letting payments update on their own.

Before, every payment update meant five platforms had to upgrade and redeploy. Now payments release on their own.

14+payment methods
5platforms
FX + DCCbrought in-house

My roleI set the technical direction and moved the platforms over.

Read the full payments story

Payments used to be a microfrontend built into each platform. So every time payments changed, each platform had to take the new version and redeploy.

We moved payments into our own Hosted Payment Page. Now each platform simply hands customers over to it, and the payments team can update it on their own.

That means new payment methods and FX or DCC changes no longer need every booking or servicing platform to release at the same time.

My role: I set the technical direction and worked with the payments team and each platform team to move them over.

Scoot · Loyalty

Paying for Scoot flights with KrisFlyer miles.

Paying with miles looks like one button. Behind it, half a dozen systems have to agree.

Millionsof miles redeemed
Flights + extrasredeemable with miles

My roleI led the web engineering and the work with the systems behind it.

Read the full loyalty story

Paying with miles sounds like a simple website feature. But it touches seat availability, fares, add-ons, fulfilment, refunds and day-to-day operations. For Award Chart and Spontaneous Escape, all of these had to work together as one smooth booking.

I enjoy problems like this, where the technical design and the business rules have to be worked out together. The customer should just see one simple booking, even though several airline and loyalty systems are involved.

My role: I led the web engineering and the connections to other systems, turning the loyalty and business rules into something the team could build and run.
